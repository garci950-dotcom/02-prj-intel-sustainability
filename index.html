2020

A shared direction

RISE strategy sets 2030 goals

Intel introduces its RISE strategy: Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling. Its 2030 goals set a direction for climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

Explore the commitment

The goals include net positive water, zero waste to landfill, and 100% renewable electricity across global operations by 2030.

2022

A climate commitment

Net-zero operations by 2040

Intel announces a goal to reach net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations, covering Scope 1 and 2 emissions.

Explore the commitment

The goal builds on efforts to reduce direct emissions from operations and indirect emissions from purchased energy.

2023

Reported progress

99% renewable electricity

Intel reports using 99% renewable electricity worldwide, a milestone on the way to its 2030 renewable electricity goal.

Explore the progress

Renewable electricity is one part of Intel's broader climate strategy, alongside reducing emissions from its operations.

2030

Goals to reach

Intel's 2030 goals bring together water, waste, and clean electricity across its global operations.

Explore the 2030 goals

Intel aims to achieve net positive water, send zero waste to landfill, and use 100% renewable electricity globally.
